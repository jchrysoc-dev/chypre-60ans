---
title: Money, plugs and practical things
summary: Euro currency, UK-style three-pin plugs, and Cyprus time (UTC+3 in May).
order: 30
---

- **Currency:** euro (EUR). Cards are widely accepted; carry some cash for villages.
- **Plugs:** UK-style three-pin (type G), 230 V. Bring an adapter if needed.
- **Time zone:** Cyprus is on UTC+3 in May (one hour ahead of Paris, two ahead of London).
- **Weather:** late May is usually warm and sunny. Bring sun cream and a hat.
