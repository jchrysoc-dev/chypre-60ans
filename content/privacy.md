---
title: Your privacy
summary: What personal information this site keeps, who can see it, and when it is deleted.
---

This is a private site for invited guests only. It is hidden from search engines.

- **What we keep:** your name and email (so you can log in), your RSVP, the
  activities you sign up for, and anything you choose to share on the
  Participants page.
- **Who sees it:** your contact details appear on the Participants page **only
  if you switch them on**, and only other logged-in guests can see them. Dietary
  needs and flight details are seen only by your household and the organisers.
- **Your choices:** you can change, hide or delete your information at any time
  from the "My details" page.
- **Deletion:** all personal information is deleted by **31 August 2027**.
